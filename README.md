# All About Tinned Fish
### Explore the charts here: https://annielee0313.github.io/tinnedfish/finals/ 

Charcuterie boards have always been my dinner party MVP, but lately, the stars have shifted from the meats and cheeses to the vibrant, flavorful world of tinned fish. I love how unique each tin is and the flavor combinations they offer -  here is a compilation of interactive graphs exploring their origins, flavors, affordability, and more using the data from Rainbow Tomatoes Garden. 

### The Process

The site design is inspired by historical and contemporary tinned fish packaging, keeping things playful and unserious.
I manually picked out and tweaked the colors for the charts. With so many fish types, I picked out a diverging palette that is harmonious, modern, and has enough contrast. 
<img width="1482" alt="design inspo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a427e135-313d-4984-a595-779038f9532e" />

The dataset is a csv file from [Rainbow Tomatoes Garden](https://rtg.fish/choosing-a-tin/), an online fish tin shop. It has over 800 rows of data that I cleaned and normalized.
